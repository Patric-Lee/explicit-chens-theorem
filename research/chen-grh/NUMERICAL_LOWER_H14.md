# Numerical Chen lower count below double exp(14)

The released theorem is `ChenEffective.GRH14.weighted_lower` in
[AuditGRH14NumericalLower.lean](AuditGRH14NumericalLower.lean). With

\[
N_*=3^{1\,000\,000}<\exp(\exp(14)),\qquad
M(N)=\mathfrak S(N)\frac{N}{(\log N)^2},
\]

it proves, for every even natural number `N >= N_*`,

\[
W(N)\ge 2.02575\,M(N).
\]

Here `W(N)` is the repository's actual `jurkatRichertWeightedCount`, not a
new proxy count. The sole added axiom is full Dirichlet GRH,
`ChenEffective.GRH.alpoge_dirichlet_grh`. The certified product window is
supplied by its existing proof; there is no remaining window hypothesis in
the released statement.

**This is a lower-count theorem, not a completed H<14 Chen existence
theorem.** The complete Selberg-square/triple estimate at this cutoff is
still needed for existence. The existing completed Chen existence theorem
at H approximately 28.82 is retained.

## Counting assembly

The source base and medium-prime estimates are

\[
\#A_N\ge (C_{\rm base}-0.06)M(N),\qquad
Q_1(N)\le (C_{\rm medium}+1.11)M(N).
\]

The actual boundary/proper-power loss is at most `M(N)/20000`. The proved
original main-coefficient inequality supplies `2.6408`, so the final
allocation is exactly

\[
2.6408-0.06-\frac{1.11}{2}-\frac1{20000}=2.02575.
\]

The generic counting assembly is in
[GRH14WeightedLower.lean](GRH14WeightedLower.lean). Its base theorem is in
[GRH14BaseCandidates.lean](GRH14BaseCandidates.lean), and the complete
medium-prime aggregate is in
[GRH14MediumAggregate.lean](GRH14MediumAggregate.lean).

## Truncated pre-sieving and numerical remainders

Keep the existing certified product start at 4,000,000 and put

\[
C=\lfloor N^{1/200}\rfloor,\qquad
D_q=\left\lfloor\frac{N^{249/500}}{2qC}\right\rfloor+1.
\]

The rounded geometry proves `q*C*D_q <= N^(249/500)`. The ratio loss
relative to `5 - 10 log(q)/log(N)` is at most `0.071`.

The exact pre-sieve expansion is truncated at `C`. Its tail uses

\[
\sum_{a\mid Q}a^{-1/2}\le e^{1700},\qquad
\sum_{a\mid Q}\sqrt a\,\nu(a)\le e^{2126},\qquad
\frac{e^{2126}}{\sqrt C}\le e^{-0.00037\log N}.
\]

Positive-multiple counting has no additive endpoint term, and the divisor
aggregation costs at most `(1+log D)^2`. Both the base truncated remainder
and the aggregate medium-prime truncated remainder are at most

\[
3\cdot10^{-12}\frac{N}{(\log N)^2}.
\]

The medium-prime argument retains the non-reduced cases `q | N`, the
`1/(q-1)` normalization, and the exceptional source-prime corrections.

## Sieve profiles and source mass

The lower factor is controlled after multiplication by the Mertens
factor. This preserves the cancellation

\[
(20e^{-\gamma})(2e^\gamma)=40.
\]

For the upper-factor shift and hat contribution, 64 rational cells give
kernel-checked majorants. The [coefficients are generated](tools/GenerateMediumKernelCertificates.mjs) with exact
integer/rational arithmetic and proved in Lean using `norm_num`; generation
does not supply an axiom or a trusted numerical oracle. There is no
`native_decide`. The resulting prime-sum bounds are `1/6` and `11/20`.

The source-mass estimates use the already proved sharp Mertens logarithmic
error. The reciprocal-tolerance parameter `r` now enters its threshold as
`sqrt(6*r)+2`, rather than linearly as `6*r+1`. This allows the required
lower mass to apply at `N_*`.
